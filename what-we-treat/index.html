How we can help

What we treat

A plain-language look at the speech, occupational therapy, literacy, and social communication areas our paediatric team commonly supports - so you can see if we might be a fit for your child.

Select a topic to read a short overview. Every child’s plan is individualized after we learn about your goals. We do not diagnose medical or neurodevelopmental conditions such as autism or ADHD, but we gladly support communication, regulation, motor, and literacy goals for children who have those diagnoses or related needs.

This page is a guide, not a complete clinical list. If you do not see your concern named, contact us - we are happy to talk through next steps.

Speech & language

Explore speech therapy for assessment and ongoing support.

Speech Sound Disorders

Support for children who have trouble producing speech sounds clearly - so listeners can understand them more easily in everyday conversation.

  • Articulation and speech clarity practice
  • Age-appropriate sound development
  • Home strategies caregivers can use
Language Disorders

Addressing expressive and receptive language - building vocabulary, sentence structure, and comprehension so children can understand others and share their ideas.

  • Expressive language therapy
  • Receptive language therapy
  • Vocabulary and sentence building
Stuttering & Fluency

Gentle, child-friendly support for stuttering and other fluency differences, with strategies that reduce pressure and build confident communication.

  • Fluency-friendly speaking strategies
  • Caregiver coaching for supportive responses
  • School and home carryover ideas
Childhood Apraxia of Speech (Motor Speech)

Help for children who know what they want to say but have difficulty planning and coordinating the movements for speech.

  • Motor planning for speech sounds
  • Practice that builds clarity step by step
  • Family coaching between sessions
Early Language & Late Talkers

Early support for infants and toddlers who are late to talk or need help building first words, gestures, and early understanding - starting from where your child is now.

  • First words and early phrases
  • Play-based language stimulation
  • Caregiver coaching in daily routines
Social Communication

Support for using language in social situations - joining play, taking turns in conversation, understanding cues, and connecting with peers.

  • Conversation and play skills
  • Reading social cues in context
  • Goals shared with families and teachers
AAC (Augmentative & Alternative Communication)

Support for children who benefit from tools that supplement or support spoken language - from simple systems to high-tech options - so every child has a way to communicate.

  • Finding a communication system that fits
  • Modelling and everyday use
  • Collaboration with families and schools
Voice

Support for children with voice concerns such as hoarseness or inefficient voice use, with practical strategies that protect the voice while keeping communication enjoyable.

  • Healthy voice habits
  • Child-friendly voice strategies
  • Guidance for home and school

Occupational therapy

Learn more on our occupational therapy page.

Sensory Processing & Regulation

Help for children who are over- or under-responsive to sensory input, or who need support to stay calm, organised, and ready to learn and play.

  • Regulation as a foundation for daily skills
  • Sensory-motor strategies for home and school
  • Tools that fit your child’s routines
Fine & Gross Motor / Motor Coordination

Support for coordination, strength, and motor planning so children can play, move, and participate more confidently in everyday activities.

  • Fine motor foundations
  • Gross motor and coordination practice
  • Motor planning for play and self-care
Handwriting

Practical support for handwriting readiness and written output - posture, pencil grasp, letter formation, and stamina - so writing feels more manageable.

  • Pre-writing and letter formation
  • Grasp, posture, and visual-motor skills
  • Strategies that carry into the classroom
Feeding & Picky Eating

Occupational therapy support for picky eating and mealtime participation - building comfort, skills, and positive routines around food without pressure.

  • Mealtime participation and comfort
  • Oral-motor and sensory approaches as appropriate
  • Family-friendly mealtime strategies
Daily Living Skills & Independence

Support for dressing, hygiene, organisation, and other self-care skills that help children participate more independently at home and school.

  • Dressing and self-care routines
  • Organisation and daily participation
  • Caregiver coaching for home practice

Literacy

See our literacy services for more detail.

Reading, Spelling & Writing

Structured literacy support for children who need a clearer path with phonological awareness, decoding, spelling, reading fluency, and written expression.

  • Phonological awareness and decoding
  • Reading fluency and comprehension foundations
  • Spelling and written expression support

Groups & family

Ask about current social groups or reach out via contact.

Social Groups

Small, clinician-facilitated groups where children practise conversation, play, and friendship skills with peers in a warm, structured setting.

  • Peer practice matched by age and goals
  • Turn-taking, conversation, and play skills
  • Clear targets shared with caregivers
Parent & Caregiver Education

We partner with families in every service - coaching caregivers on strategies that fit real life at home, daycare, and school so progress can continue between visits.

  • Practical strategies for daily routines
  • Clear goals and progress updates
  • Interdisciplinary teamwork when helpful
