Speech-language pathology

Speech therapy for children

Assessment and therapy for speech sounds, language, fluency, social communication, and related needs - delivered in a child-friendly clinic.

How speech therapy helps

Our speech-language pathologists work with children who need support with how they speak, understand, and use language with others.

  • Speech sound development and clarity
  • Expressive and receptive language
  • Fluency / stuttering support
  • Social communication skills
  • Parent coaching and home strategies

Sessions are play-based and goal-focused. We share progress clearly so you always know what we are working on and why.
