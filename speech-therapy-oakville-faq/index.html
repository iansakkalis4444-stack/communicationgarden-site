FAQ

Speech therapy & clinic FAQ

Quick answers about services, booking, funding, and what to expect at Communication Garden in Oakville.

What ages do you see?

We primarily support children. Reach out with your child’s age and goals and we will confirm fit and next steps.

How do I book?

Book online through Jane, call 647-451-0188, email info@communicationgarden.ca, or use our contact form.

Where are you located?

We are at 407A Speers Rd, Oakville, ON L6K 3T5.

What are your hours?

Every day 8:00 am – 9:00 pm.

Do you accept OAP funding?

Many services can be funded through Ontario Autism Program childhood budgets. See our OAP funding page or ask us when you enquire.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any prior reports, school notes, and funding information if relevant. Wear clothes your child can move and play in.

Do caregivers stay in the session?

Often yes for younger children — we also coach caregivers so strategies carry over at home. Your clinician will discuss what works best.

Still have a question? Contact us or call 647-451-0188.
