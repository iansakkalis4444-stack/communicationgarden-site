Occupational therapy

Occupational therapy for kids

Support for fine motor skills, sensory regulation, self-care, and participation at home, school, and in the community.

What OT can address

  • Fine motor and handwriting foundations
  • Sensory processing and regulation
  • Self-care routines (dressing, feeding independence)
  • Attention, organization, and daily participation
  • Play skills and motor planning

Therapy is individualized and practical - we focus on skills that matter in everyday life, with caregiver coaching woven in.
