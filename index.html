About us

Communication Garden is a paediatric clinic in Oakville offering speech-language pathology, occupational therapy, literacy support, and social groups.

We believe every child can grow stronger communication and daily living skills when therapy feels safe, playful, and connected to family life.

Goals are clear, progress is shared, and caregivers are partners in every step.

Our approach

A calm, colourful space where children build skills through play and purposeful activities.

01

Child-centred and play-based

Sessions feel like play while targeting clear goals you can see progress on.

02

Family partnership

We coach caregivers so strategies carry over to home, daycare, and school.

03

Convenient Oakville location

Easy access on Speers Road with evening and weekend hours.

04

Funding-aware care

We support families using Ontario Autism Program (OAP) and private benefits where eligible.

Our services

Four core services for communication, movement, reading, and friendship skills - tailored to each child.

Speech Therapy

Assessment and therapy for speech sounds, language, fluency, social communication, and feeding-related needs.

Learn more →

Occupational Therapy

Support for fine motor skills, sensory regulation, self-care, and participation at home and school.

Learn more →

Literacy Services

Structured literacy support to build reading, spelling, and writing confidence.

Small-group sessions that practise conversation, play, and friendship skills in a supportive setting.

Ontario Autism Program (OAP)

Many of our services can be funded through OAP childhood budgets. We can help you understand what documentation you need and how booking works.

  • Speech-language pathology and occupational therapy
  • Invoices and reports suited to funding workflows
  • Clear next steps if you are new to OAP
