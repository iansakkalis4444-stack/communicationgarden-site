Funding

Ontario Autism Program (OAP) funding

Many Communication Garden services can be paid with OAP childhood budgets. We help families understand documentation and booking steps.

How we support OAP families

  • Speech-language pathology and occupational therapy that may be OAP-eligible
  • Clear invoices and clinical documentation for funding workflows
  • Guidance if you are new to using childhood budgets for therapy

OAP rules and eligible services can change. We recommend confirming your child’s budget details with your OAP contact, and we will work with you on clinic-side paperwork.

Next steps

  1. Contact us or book online and mention OAP.
  2. Share any existing assessment or funding letters you have.
  3. We outline services, estimates, and documentation needs.
