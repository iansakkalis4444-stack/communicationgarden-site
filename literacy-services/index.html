Literacy

Literacy services

Structured support to build reading, spelling, and writing confidence — for children who need a clearer path to literacy success.

Building strong readers and writers

Our literacy services complement speech and OT by targeting foundational reading and writing skills with structured, evidence-informed approaches.

  • Phonological awareness and decoding
  • Reading fluency and comprehension foundations
  • Spelling and written expression support
  • Strategies families can practise at home
