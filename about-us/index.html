About the clinic

Growing communication in Oakville

Communication Garden is a paediatric clinic offering speech-language pathology, occupational therapy, literacy support, and social groups in a warm, garden-inspired space at 407A Speers Rd, Oakville, ON L6K 3T5.

Our approach

We believe every child can grow stronger communication and daily living skills when therapy feels safe, playful, and connected to family life. Goals are clear, progress is shared, and caregivers are partners in every step.

What families can expect

  • Assessment and therapy tailored to your child
  • Practical strategies for home and school
  • Transparent communication about goals and progress
  • Support navigating OAP and private benefits when relevant

Louise Dionne is a Registered Occupational Therapist with a Master of Science in Occupational Therapy and over five years of experience supporting children and families in pediatric settings. She began her career working closely with children with autism spectrum disorder, sensory processing differences, ADHD, developmental delays, and motor coordination challenges, which shaped her clinical approach and passion for pediatric care.

Louise has worked across clinic, school, and home-based environments, supporting infants and children from birth through the early school years. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology with a minor in Psychology, and her graduate research explored the effectiveness of social stories in supporting self-regulation in children with autism.

She has completed additional professional training in sensory integration, reflex integration, Handwriting Without Tears, and the Sensory Profile-2, allowing her to thoughtfully support children with sensory-motor, regulation, and functional participation needs. Louise brings a strong focus on regulation as the foundation for learning, movement, and daily life skills, and values practical strategies that can be consistently carried over across home, school, and therapy settings.

In her current role as Head Occupational Therapist, Louise leads and supervises the OT team, providing mentorship, clinical guidance, and oversight to ensure high-quality, consistent care. Through years of hands-on experience, she has learned that meaningful progress happens when children are supported by aligned teams and when families feel confident, informed, and empowered. She places a strong emphasis on parent education, interdisciplinary collaboration, and clinician support.

Outside of her professional role, Louise values time with her family and daughter. She also enjoys yoga, travel, and reading. She brings curiosity, structure, and compassion into both her work and everyday life, and remains deeply committed to ongoing learning and professional growth.
