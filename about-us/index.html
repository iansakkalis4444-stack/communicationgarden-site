About the clinic

Growing communication in Oakville

Communication Garden is a paediatric clinic offering speech-language pathology, occupational therapy, literacy support, and social groups in a warm, garden-inspired space at 407A Speers Rd, Oakville, ON L6K 3T5.

Our approach

We believe every child can grow stronger communication and daily living skills when therapy feels safe, playful, and connected to family life. Goals are clear, progress is shared, and caregivers are partners in every step.

What families can expect

  • Assessment and therapy tailored to your child
  • Practical strategies for home and school
  • Transparent communication about goals and progress
  • Support navigating OAP and private benefits when relevant
