Social skills

Social groups

Small-group sessions where children practise conversation, play, and friendship skills in a supportive, structured setting.

Learn together

Social groups give children a chance to practise skills with peers — taking turns, reading social cues, starting conversations, and joining play.

  • Small groups matched by age and goals
  • Facilitated by clinicians
  • Clear targets shared with caregivers
  • A warm, garden-themed clinic environment

Availability varies by season. Contact us or book online to ask about current groups.

Book / enquire Email info@communicationgarden.ca
